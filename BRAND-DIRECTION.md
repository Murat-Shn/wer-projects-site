# WeR Projects — Brand Direction

## Core idea

WeR Projects is an independent product studio where curiosity becomes working software. The name carries two meanings: “we are” signals a human, open attitude; “projects” leaves room for apps, games, tools and experiments.

## Character

- Curious without looking random
- Technical without feeling cold
- Playful without appearing childish
- Independent, practical and quietly confident
- Focused on making ideas tangible

## Design thesis

**Connected ideas, made real.** The visual system uses modules, paths and changing directions to express separate thoughts becoming one coherent product. It avoids space, atom and generic AI imagery.

## Visual language

- A light, paper-like base with near-black typography
- Electric blue and warm coral as precise, energetic accents
- Large editorial type paired with compact technical labels
- A modular connection symbol as the primary mark
- Product cards that feel like objects on a working desk
- Restrained motion based on assembly and reveal

## Logo system

The generated asset is the symbol only. The “WeR Projects” wordmark remains live text for clarity, accessibility and reliable rendering. The symbol should work at favicon size, in one color and against both light and dark surfaces.

## Voice

Short, direct and specific. WeR Projects speaks as a maker showing real work. No inflated startup language, unexplained jargon or claims unsupported by released products.
